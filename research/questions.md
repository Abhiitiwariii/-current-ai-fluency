# Research Questions

> Define what you're trying to answer before collecting sources.

## Primary question

- Does a daily **awe card → guided prompt rep → streak** loop create a genuine *return habit* for time-poor, mid-career **non-technical marketers** — enough to justify a daily open and, eventually, a subscription?

## Sub-questions (riskiest assumptions to test with the 5–15 users)

- **Aha in session 1:** Do users reach the "I produced one impressive thing" moment within the first ~60 seconds, before any signup?
- **Return:** Do they come back on D1 unprompted (habit signal), or only when nudged?
- **"Realness" of grading:** Does deterministic-only grading still *feel* like real progress and real doing, or does it read as a quiz? (If it reads as a quiz, the "I'm becoming AI-fluent" chase is false.)
- **Register fit:** Does the gamified-loop / premium-skin combo land as "serious and respects my time" for a status-sensitive adult, or does any of it read as a toy?

## Out of scope (for this MVP / test window)

- Long-term retention and subscription conversion (can't be proven in 4 days / 5–15 users).
- Personas beyond marketers; audio, offline, LLM grading, B2B/cohort, WhatsApp.
- Statistical significance — this is directional signal, not a powered experiment.

## Success criteria

What does a good answer look like? What decision does it inform?

- **Good answer:** a majority of the 5–15 users activate (complete first awe→rep loop) and a meaningful subset return D1 — plus 1–2 clear behavioural insights about *where* they drop and *why*.
- **Decision it informs:** whether the loop is worth investing in beyond the MVP (build persona 2, add awe-supply pipeline) or needs the rep/aha reworked first.
